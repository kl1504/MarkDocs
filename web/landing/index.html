Questions, answered

Who owns my content?

You do. Everything you write and publish belongs to you, and you can export it at any time.

Does it really work offline?

Yes. As a PWA, the editor and your published docs are cached so you can write and read without a connection. Publishing and payments need internet access.

How do I get paid when I sell a book?

Connect your Stripe or PayPal account and readers pay you directly, so revenue goes to you, not a marketplace. Standard payment processor fees still apply.

Can I leave with my content?

Yes. Export your Markdown source, docs site, and book files whenever you like.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. If you can write Markdown, you can publish docs and sell a book.
